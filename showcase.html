The ServeMatch story

Good intentions should not get lost in search results.

ServeMatch turns a person’s real-life constraints into explainable local recommendations—and then removes friction from every next step until they show up.

Practical details come first.

A role can sound interesting and still be impossible to reach. ServeMatch distinguishes a known conflict, an unknown requirement, and a personal preference.

  1. Start

    1. Tell us what works

    A person wants to help, but listings make them compare age rules, schedules, transportation, distance, and eligibility by hand.

  2. Match

    2. See realistic options

    Quick Match collects only four essential constraints, then filters known conflicts and ranks realistic opportunities.

  3. Compare

    3. Make a clearer choice

    Compare up to three roles by fit, travel, schedule, age, and training. Missing information stays marked for confirmation.

  4. Act

    4. Take the next step

    Open official signup, prepare editable questions, and check directions. Keep a checklist, save a shift to your calendar, and download self-reported service records. Only the volunteer marks a step complete.

A recommendation you can inspect.

Deterministic matching

TypeScript separates eligibility and scoring. Results sort by score; exact ties use explicit language evidence and a stable ID. No language model makes live eligibility decisions.

Validated data

Zod validation and automated tests protect IDs, URLs, types, score invariants, and unknown values.

Private by default

A person can match and plan without an account. Optional sign-in does not synchronize answers or plans across devices.

Action, not browsing

Official signup, personalized outreach, maps, calendar export, and a service notebook support the next steps.

A local pilot

Built to learn from real volunteers.

The next milestone is testing with local volunteers and organizations. Changes in signup time or completed service have not yet been established. Organizations confirm current openings, and checklist progress is self-reported.
