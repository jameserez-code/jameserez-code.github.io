Privacy, in plain language

You can browse and match as a guest. ServeMatch does not operate a student-record database, and your matching answers are not sent to a school or volunteer organization.

Saved in your browser

Survey answers, optional school selection, bookmarks, comparisons, action-plan items, service-hour entries, goals, and aggregate feature counters are stored on this device. If browser storage is blocked, the app keeps changes only for the current tab.

Optional account sign-in

Email/password sign-in is provided by Google Firebase. Firebase receives the account email and authentication information. Signing in does not upload or synchronize ServeMatch survey, plan, or hour data.

No external behavior analytics

ServeMatch does not currently send analytics events to a third-party analytics service. Device-local counters support testing on this browser only and cannot measure school-wide participation or unique visitors.

When you leave ServeMatch

Official signup, map, email, and organization links open external services. Those services receive normal web request information and apply their own privacy practices. ServeMatch does not control their forms or acceptance decisions.

Delete your local data

This removes ServeMatch data from this browser. It does not delete a Firebase account or records held by organizations you contacted.
