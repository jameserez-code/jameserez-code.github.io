How matching works

We look at what you told us, remove roles that clearly will not work, and put the best fits first.

The score describes preference fit, not acceptance odds. Results sort by score. When scores tie, an explicit language need can take priority, followed by a stable listing ID.

1 · Gather

Find local opportunities

We bring together local roles and link you to the organization when you are ready to sign up.

2 · Check

Make sure the basics work

We check age, distance, transportation, schedule, and any required language before showing a match.

3 · Match

Put your best fits first

Stronger matches appear first, with a short explanation of why each one may work for you.

We check the essentials first

Closed roles and clear conflicts with age, schedule, travel, or transportation are removed before you see your results.

We do not guess

If an important detail is not listed, we tell you to confirm it with the organization.

Skills can strengthen a match

Your languages and skills help bring relevant opportunities closer to the top.

A match is a starting point

A high fit score means the role lines up well with your answers. The organization still confirms availability and acceptance.
